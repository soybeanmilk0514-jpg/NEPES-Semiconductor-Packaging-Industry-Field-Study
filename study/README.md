# Study Notes

## OSAT

Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test.

OSAT companies perform packaging, assembly, and test services for semiconductor products.

## RDL

Redistribution Layer.

A metal interconnect layer that reroutes chip I/O positions to new locations.

## Bump

A conductive structure used for electrical / mechanical chip interconnection.

Examples include solder bump and Cu pillar.

## WLP

Wafer Level Package.

Packaging processes are performed before wafer dicing.

## Fan-Out

A package architecture that extends redistribution beyond the original chip area to increase available I/O.

## Developer

A lithography chemical that selectively removes the intended photoresist region after exposure to realize the pattern.

## Cu Electroplating

Electrochemical deposition of Cu used in interconnect / bump / TSV-related structures.

## SPC

Statistical Process Control.

A method for monitoring process variation using data and detecting abnormal trends before they become large production issues.

## Yield

Fraction of products meeting required specification after manufacturing / test.

## Change Point

Any production change that may affect quality, such as material, equipment, recipe, maintenance, or supplier changes.

## Torpedo Car

A rail-based insulated vessel used in steelmaking to transport molten iron between process stages.
