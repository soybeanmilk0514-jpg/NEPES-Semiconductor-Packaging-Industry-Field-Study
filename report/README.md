# Field-Study Portfolio Scope

This repository was reconstructed without a submitted report file.

Its basis is:

1. direct industrial visits to NEPES and Hyundai Steel
2. direct observation of production sites and employee explanations
3. official NEPES business / R&D pages
4. current NEPES recruitment pages for process, equipment, and quality roles
5. official Hyundai Steel process information
6. career interpretation developed during portfolio reconstruction

## Evidence Rule

### Field observation
Only statements the visitor actually experienced are described as direct observation.

### Public company fact
Company business / product / role information is described as post-visit research.

### Career interpretation
Connections to semiconductor process / manufacturing / quality jobs are labeled as personal interpretation.

No confidential company information is included.
